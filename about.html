About The Sail Project

The Sail Project is made up of two members. We've built decent experience in song-making throughout our time active in our band — with a focus on rock, spanning everything from pop-rock to heavy rock.

🎸

Tom

Guitarist / Instrumentalist

Tom is mainly a guitarist, but has experience playing other instruments too. He mainly writes the instrumentals for the band's songs, and has lately been learning to create his own vocals and lyrics as well.

🎤

Lau

Lyricist, guitarist, and vocalist.

Through poetry, Lau was able to expand his scope in writing towards music and songwriting. Though guitars are his only instruments as of now, he is looking forward to learning how to play other instruments; especially keyboards.
