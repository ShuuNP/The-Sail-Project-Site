Every musician has a song still docked at the harbor.

Maybe it's an idea with no arrangement. A voice memo from two years ago. Lyrics without a melody. An unfinished project that never quite made it to the finish line. We're here to help you bring that song to life. :)

This is a passion project by us. While our collaborations come at no cost, we will put our time, creativity, and 100% effort into making your song something we're proud to share with you.

How this works

You bring the idea. We help it sail.

Send over the seed of a song — a melody, lyrics, a reference track, even just a feeling you're chasing. We'll shape it together into something finished.

For now, we're focusing our collaborations on VTubers and content creators. Whether you need an original song, an intro, an ending theme, or simply a song you've always wanted to bring to life, we'd love to hear your idea.

This runs as a free service, in exchange for the room to build repertoire, grow as musicians, and take on work we actually believe in.

Before you apply

A few honest terms

  • One song per application — We are only taking on a limited number of projects, so each collaboration is limited to one song.
  • 2+ month turnaround — Please allow at least two months for the project. This gives us enough time to work on the songwriting, arrangement, recording, and production without rushing the creative process. Heads up: this is our confident baseline, not a fixed countdown — it's the timeframe we're comfortable committing to, not a guarantee it'll take exactly two months either way.
  • Limited revisions — A limited number of revisions is included. Major changes to the song's concept, structure, or direction after the agreed-upon arrangement may not be accommodated. Heads up: this applies as we're closing in on the finished song, not during the early stages — there's plenty of room to shape and adjust things together while we're still building it out.
  • Made by us, not AI — Every part of the creative process is personally handled by us. We guarantee that no generative AI tools will be used to create the songwriting, performances, or musical content of your song.
  • No rushed projects — We are doing this in our free time, so we cannot accommodate urgent deadlines or last-minute requests.
  • Limited availability — We will only work with 2-4 selected collaborators at a time. Submitting an application does not guarantee that your project will be accepted.
  • Style — Our primary styles are rock (Alt rock, Pop-rock, Hard Rock, Etc.. ). Acoustic-based compositions may also be considered depending on the project.

This is a collaboration, so expect contact and suggestions along the way. This is a two-way street between musicians to create music, so we should respect our responsibilities in the project.
